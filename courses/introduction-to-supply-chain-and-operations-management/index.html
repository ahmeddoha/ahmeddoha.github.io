How the learning works

Do more than recognize a formula.

Each topic follows the same discipline: understand the system, select and execute the appropriate method, then interpret the result as a business decision.

The Operations Decision Rhythm

Use this sequence for worked examples, practice sets, quizzes, and the final examination.

Step 1

Frame the system

Name the decision, customer promise, process boundary, performance measure, and information available.

A model begins with the right question.
→
Step 2

Work the model

Select the method, state assumptions and units, structure the data, calculate, and check whether the result is feasible.

Show the setup—not only the answer.
→
Step 3

Make the decision

Interpret the result, identify the driving constraint or assumption, and explain customer and system-wide consequences.

Numbers earn value when they guide action.

How your work is assessed

Assessment rewards method selection, execution, and interpretation.

The three quizzes, attendance, and non-cumulative final total 100%. Select an item to see its purpose and evidence of strong work.

10%Quiz 1

The coverage rule: topics assessed in Quiz 1, Quiz 2, or Quiz 3 are excluded from the non-cumulative final. Brightspace provides current delivery instructions and the University publishes the final-exam schedule.

The visible quality standard

A calculation is not finished until it becomes a decision.

Strong work identifies the right method, shows the setup and units, calculates accurately, checks assumptions, and interprets the operating consequence.

The same evidence chain applies to qualitative questions: claim, course concept, operating evidence, trade-off, and recommendation.

1DecisionWhat must the operation decide?
2Model + inputsWhich method fits, and what do the data mean?
3Result + sensitivityWhat drives the answer, and when would it change?
