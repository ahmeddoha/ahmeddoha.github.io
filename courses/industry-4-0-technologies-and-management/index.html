How your work is assessed

Eighty percent develops and documents one proposal.

Three checkpoints, the term paper, final defense, and active participation total 100%. Select an item to see what it produces and what strong work demonstrates.

30%Three Project Checkpoints

The project rule: the checkpoints are not three unrelated presentations. They accumulate into one approximately twenty-page, double-spaced paper and one final presentation and question period. Brightspace carries rubrics, groups, formats, submissions, and deadlines.

The visible quality standard

Do not jump from a sensor to “business value.”

Strong work makes the intermediate reasoning visible: event, stakeholder pain, data, analysis, insight, decision, response, outcome, business logic, and risk.

If one link is vague or unsupported, the project should say what evidence or test is needed rather than hide the uncertainty.

1Problem + stakeholderWhich measurable pain matters to whom?
2Sensor + dataWhat must be observed, at what quality and speed?
3Analysis + actionWhich insight changes which accountable decision?
4Value + viabilityWho benefits, who pays, and what can block adoption?
